Badge Math

The 240 attendees at 25 badges a pack take 10 packs, the last pack holding 15 badges.

Boundary: arithmetic only Badge Math counts an event's badges with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the packs the badges take, the lanyard bags the attendees take, the cost an attendee, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your badges, your packs or your printer and does not know whether the badges will really arrive on time, the printer will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pack is worth its price or a badge is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 240 attendees, 25 badges a pack, 50 lanyards a bag, $12.75 a pack and 8 badges a minute.

The lanyards truth

The 240 attendees at 50 lanyards a bag take 5 bags, leaving 10 lanyards spare.

The cost truth

The 10 packs at $12.75 a pack come to $127.50, $0.53 an attendee.

The time truth

The 240 badges at 8 badges a minute take 30 minutes, 0 hours 30 minutes.
