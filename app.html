Badge Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's badges with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the packs the badges take, the lanyard bags the attendees take, the cost an attendee, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. The attendees, the pack size, the bag size, the pack price and the printing speed are yours to decide and read off your own registration lists, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your badges, your packs or your printer and does not know whether the badges will really arrive on time, the printer will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pack is worth its price or a badge is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The packs truth

Packs: the attendees divided by the badges a pack, rounded up, with what the last pack holds.

The lanyards truth

Lanyards: the attendees divided by the lanyards a bag, rounded up, with the lanyards left spare.

The cost truth

Cost: the packs times the pack price, to the cent, and spread over the attendees.

The time truth

Time: the badges divided by the badges printed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Badge Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
